SF Inspector

Salesforce tools for Safari

  1. Turn the extension on

    Safari keeps new extensions switched off until you say otherwise.

    The extension is on. You can turn it off again in Safari’s settings whenever you like.

  2. Allow it on your Salesforce sites

    In the same panel, set SF Inspector to Always Allow on your Salesforce domains. Without this it loads but cannot read anything, and behaves as though you were signed out.
